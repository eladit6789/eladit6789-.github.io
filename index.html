About Me

I am a PhD student in the Biomolecular Sciences program at the Weizmann Institute of Science, working in Prof. Rivka Dikstein's laboratory.

My research focuses on understanding how cells regulate gene expression at the level of translation.

Research Interests

My PhD project, "Identification and characterization of cellular NSP1-like translational regulatory proteins," investigates how the ribosome's mRNA entry channel contributes to translational control.

I am particularly interested in the interactions between ribosomal proteins and regulatory factors, and how these interactions may connect translation with cellular stress and innate immune responses.

  • Translation regulation and ribosome biology
  • Ribosomal protein interactions
  • Translational control during cellular stress
  • Translation and innate immunity
